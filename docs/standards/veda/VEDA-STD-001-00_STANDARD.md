# VEDA-STD-001 Universal Knowledge, Retrieval, Learning & Response Standard

Status: AUTHORISED

VEDA must know the research it is authorised to use, label every item by trust, retrieve according to context, preserve conflicts, and promote to Approved Core only through P010/Admin governance.

Trust labels control authority, confidence, presentation, and activation. They do not suppress research, retrieval, experimentation, shadow execution, backtesting, ML, or knowledge comparison.

This standard is inherited by all future VEDA phases. P027 is not started by this change.
