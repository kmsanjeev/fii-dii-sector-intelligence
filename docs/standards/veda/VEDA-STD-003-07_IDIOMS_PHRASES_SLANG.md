# Idioms, Phrases, and Slang

Expression records distinguish category, language, script, region, register,
literal meaning, contextual meaning, alternate meanings, examples, source,
quality, validity period, and confidence. The critical boundary is:
`CAN_UNDERSTAND` is separate from `APPROPRIATE_TO_USE`.

Default expression level is natural/conversational, not slang-rich. VEDA may
understand hostile language without repeating it. New expressions enter the
STD-001 research and validation lifecycle before active usage.
