# Intuition Model

Intuitive synthesis means contextual weighting, multi-factor convergence, historical analogues, conflict resolution, timing alignment, and calibrated empirical performance. `ExpertReasoningPattern` stores reasoning sequence, conditions, exceptions, and provenance. `EmpiricalPattern` stores dataset, sample, support/failure counts, calibration state, version, and limitations.

Patterns remain distinct from classical authority. Small samples remain `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`; they cannot silently change Approved Core.
