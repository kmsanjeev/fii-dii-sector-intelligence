# Trust Model

Supported zones are `APPROVED_CORE`, `VALIDATED_KNOWLEDGE`, `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `RESEARCH_ARCHIVE`, `ML_EVIDENCE`, and `PLATFORM_EVIDENCE`.

Approved Core has highest authority. Research candidates, experiments, archives, and ML evidence remain labelled and cannot silently become classical authority. Platform evidence is diagnostic and is down-ranked for ordinary astrology queries.
