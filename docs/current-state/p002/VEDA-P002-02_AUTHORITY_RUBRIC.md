# VEDA-P002-02 Authority Rubric

## Rubric Shape

The authority model is multi-dimensional. P002 does not collapse provenance into one popularity score.

Tracked dimensions:

- `textual_authority`
- `traditional_authority`
- `translation_reliability`
- `cross_source_support`
- `empirical_support`
- `implementation_confidence`

## Tier Meaning

- `TIER_A`: highest classical authority in a usable verified form
- `TIER_B`: strong translation / commentary / edition support
- `TIER_C`: respected but weaker interpretive support
- `TIER_D`: academic or empirical support
- `TIER_E`: internal or tertiary synthesis
- `TIER_F`: weak informal support
- `TIER_U`: unknown

## Current Pilot Reality

- BPHS, Hora Sara, and Brihat Jataka are treated as the strongest pilot authorities
- Phaladeepika, Saravali, and Jataka Parijata are registered but still metadata-level in this phase
- internal synthesis is explicitly lower authority than classical sources
