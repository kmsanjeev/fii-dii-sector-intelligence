# Limitations

- The 114-subject frame is an ADB source-governed birth pool; raw ADB data is
  local/ignored and is not committed.
- The four events are a small public-office pilot and are not powered.
- Birth-source dependence is concentrated in one cluster.
- No India candidate qualified in this frame.
- Exact role dates do not establish predictive validity.
- Control dates are preregistered design metadata, not tested controls.
- Death endpoints, month/year-only claims and generic career endings remain
  excluded from the primary cohort.
- Wikipedia and generic biography pages were discovery-only and were not used
  as final event authority.
- No astrology, feature, ML, PRED-M4 or production activity occurred.
