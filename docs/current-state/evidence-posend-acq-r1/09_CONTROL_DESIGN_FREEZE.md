# Control design freeze

The pilot metadata freezes a within-role, pre-event design:

- two deterministic dates per event, at one-third and two-thirds of the
  frozen role duration;
- both dates must be strictly inside the active role interval and before the
  event date;
- known competing position-end events are excluded;
- no post-event dates are allowed;
- calendar month/weekday matching is not applied until a future estimand
  decision explicitly preregisters it.

Eight control dates were generated for the four exact-day events. They are
design metadata only. No chart, feature activation, association, or model was
calculated. A future design-freeze programme must finalize the estimand,
matching strategy, multiplicity and power before any feature study.
