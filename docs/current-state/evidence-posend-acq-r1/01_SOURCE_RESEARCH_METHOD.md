# Source research method

The source pass was conducted after the 114-record birth frame was frozen.
Candidate discovery was identity/role-first and did not inspect any chart or
feature output. Public discovery indexes were used only to locate likely
institutional records. Final evidence was accepted from the French National
Assembly, the European Commission's Belgian representation, the Italian
Senate, and the associated institutional records listed in
`05_EVENT_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.json`.

Accepted exact-day claims:

- Nicole Catala: official French National Assembly mandate, 1997-06-01 to
  2002-06-18.
- Pierre Cardo: official French National Assembly mandate, 2002-06-19 to
  2007-06-19.
- Willy de Clercq: European Commission role, 1985-01-06 to 1989-01-05.
- Vittorino Colombo: Italian Senate presidency, 1983-05-12 to 1983-07-11.

The source registry stores URLs, publisher/source class, retrieval date and
the bounded claim. No full page text or copyrighted biography was copied.
Month/year-only retirement evidence and death endpoints were retained as
screening outcomes, not promoted to the primary corpus.
