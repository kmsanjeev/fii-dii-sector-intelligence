# VEDA-P010 Conflict and High-Stakes Policy

Promotion does not erase contradiction.

Implemented conflict behavior:
- attach `conflict_ids` to promoted claims and rules;
- allow `PROMOTED_WITH_CONDITIONS` when unresolved contradiction remains governed but acceptable;
- preserve scope and conditionality rather than collapsing disagreement into a synthesized truth.

High-stakes policy survives promotion for:
- `HEALTH`
- `LONGEVITY`
- `DEATH`
- `FERTILITY`
- `FINANCE`
- `REMEDIES`

Implemented high-stakes controls:
- explicit preflight warning/conditioning;
- continued output-boundary separation from P005-R1;
- no automatic relaxation of user-facing safeguards after knowledge promotion.
