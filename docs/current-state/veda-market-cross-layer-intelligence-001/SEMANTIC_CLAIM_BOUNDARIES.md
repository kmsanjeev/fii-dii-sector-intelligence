# Semantic claim boundaries

Allowed: “Broader institutional positioning is cautious while Energy is
leading and selected stocks are outperforming the sector.”

Not allowed: “FIIs are buying Energy,” “FIIs are accumulating RELIANCE,”
“smart money entered Banking,” BUY/SELL instructions, target prices or price
forecasts without separately authorized evidence and capability.

“Smart money” is user-facing shorthand for institutional/professional
participant evidence plus observable Market leadership. It does not assert
that FII is always smart, PRO is always correct, or CLIENT is always wrong.
