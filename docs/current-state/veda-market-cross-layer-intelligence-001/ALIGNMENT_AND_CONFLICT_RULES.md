# Alignment and conflict rules

- Market and institutional states disagreeing produces a material conflict.
- Leading/improving sector plus strong stock produces `STOCK_SECTOR_ALIGNED`.
- Leading sector plus weak stock produces `STOCK_LAGS_LEADING_SECTOR`.
- Weakening/lagging sector plus strong stock produces
  `STOCK_OUTPERFORMS_WEAK_SECTOR`; this is divergence, not a bullish claim.
- Strong stock without confirming volume produces a minor conflict.
- Non-aligned source dates produce a date limitation/conflict.
- Missing layers produce insufficient evidence and lower quality; they do not
  become neutral or zero.
