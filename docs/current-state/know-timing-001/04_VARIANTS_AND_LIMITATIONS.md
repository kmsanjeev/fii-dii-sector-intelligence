## Translation and historical limitations

Translations use terms such as “acquisition,” “progress,” “religious-mindedness,”
“journey,” “loss,” and “success” at different levels of specificity. These do
not automatically map to modern events like a signed employment contract,
degree completion, immigration status, or legal ownership transfer.

The texts also reflect historical institutions, kingship, ritual, family and
social roles. Modern event definitions must remain separate from the textual
result language.

## Rejected extensions

- Generic Dasha recipes with no source passage.
- “Transit alone creates the event” formulas.
- Modern exact-date claims derived from broad Dasha results.
- Public-office, career-start, education-completion, relocation and property
  signals assembled from unrelated significations.
- Empirical case-fit selection of a preferred textual variant.
