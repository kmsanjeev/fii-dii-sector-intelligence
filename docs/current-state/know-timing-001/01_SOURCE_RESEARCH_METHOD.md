# VEDA-KNOW-TIMING-001 — Source Research Method

## Authority and access

The primary inspected translation was the [Brihat Parashara Hora
Shastra](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf),
especially Chapters 47–51 and the Dasha/Antardasha results on pp. 88–98.
The inspected [Phaladeepika translation](https://www.jyotishvidya.com/HTMLobj-9415/Mantreswara_s__Phaladeeplka_.pdf)
covered Chapters 16–20 and pp. 161–182. The [Sanskrit Documents Jyotisha
catalogue](https://sanskritdocuments.org/iast/jyotisha/) was used to locate
Sanskrit and scan witnesses for Bṛhat Jātaka, Jātaka Pārijāta and BPHS.
The [Jātaka Pārijāta Sanskrit edition](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/jAtakaparijAtaH.html)
is encoded/proofread source material but was not treated as an English
passage-level timing translation.

Saravali, Hora Sara, Uttara Kalamrita, Brihat Jataka and Laghu Parashari were
reviewed as candidate source families through accessible catalogue, scan or
translation metadata. Where the exact event-timing passage was not reliably
verified in the accessed material, the result is `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED`, not
a fabricated citation.

## Classification rules

- `PRIMARY_CLASSICAL`: direct text or reliable translation inspected.
- `CLASSICAL_COMMENTARY`: attributed commentary or explanatory translation.
- `PRACTITIONER_TRADITION`: attributable later method, never silently primary.
- `MODERN_INTERPRETATION`: discovery only.
- `NATAL_ONLY`, `TIMING_PARTIAL`, and `TIMING_EXPLICIT` are kept separate.
