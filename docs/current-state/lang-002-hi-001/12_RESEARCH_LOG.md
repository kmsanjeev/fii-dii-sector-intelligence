# Research Log

The work was a bounded implementation and governance activity, not a new
Jyotisha source-research phase. Existing VEDA localization/STD-003 materials
and the canonical term registry were audited first. The parent English pack,
its 33 term IDs, 16 messages, fallback behavior, and review metadata were
inspected before implementation.

Accepted inputs: the governed canonical registry, inherited ontology source
paths, STD-003 Hindi language boundary, and the HI-001 programme specification.
Rejected as authority: SEO pages, unsourced translation tables, automatic
runtime translation, and any unreviewed claim of human linguistic validation.

Unresolved: regional Hindi variants, independent linguistic/source review, and
production approval. No RAG rebuild or external provider call was required.
