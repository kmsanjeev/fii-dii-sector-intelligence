# P031 Safety and Boundaries

Blocked outputs include enlightenment, moksha, liberation, sainthood, siddhi, formal-renunciation certainty, guru-selection advice, religious-authority claims, and clinical or mental-health diagnosis.

Spiritual crisis may describe questioning, inward turning, existential reorientation or detachment from prior values. It never diagnoses depression, psychosis, mania, trauma disorder or any other clinical condition.

P031 does not prescribe religion, meditation, ritual, retreat, guru, seva, or life decisions. It reports conditional orientation and alternatives from supplied governed evidence.
