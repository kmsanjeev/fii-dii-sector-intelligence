# Domain Integration

P027 accepts evidence from Career, Wealth, Education, Marriage, Progeny, and Health aggregators without rewriting them. Health and progeny limitations remain attached to their evidence. Cross-domain relationships are represented as associations, not unsupported causal claims.

PRED consumers can use dominant scenario, alternatives, timing, confidence, and evidence-for/against as a future handoff. EMP-001 remains `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`; P027 does not upgrade predictive maturity.
