# House, Planet and Varga Governance

The P030 registry labels 3rd, 7th, 9th and 12th-house movement claims as research candidates, while 4th-house residence context is reference-level. Rahu, Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Mercury and Venus movement claims remain research candidates; Moon has reference-level home/mobility context.

D4 calculation is validated by P015-RX, but D4 interpretation remains not validated. D9, D10 and D12 travel interpretations remain disabled.
