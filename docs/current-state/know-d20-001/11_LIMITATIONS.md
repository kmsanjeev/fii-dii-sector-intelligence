# Limitations

1. The accessible BPHS evidence gives a narrow D20 purpose and deity lists,
   not a complete interpretive manual.
2. Translation and edition differences may affect `upāsanā`, deity names and
   the relation between category starts and destination mapping.
3. The current sign progression is evidence-qualified, not fully passage-
   proven.
4. No D20 empirical fixtures or longitudinal outcomes exist.
5. D20 is birth-time sensitive, especially for the Ascendant and boundaries.
6. Classical religious and social context must not be universalized into
   modern religious, gender or monastic claims.
7. This activity does not validate D9, D24, D60, Dasha or Transit spiritual
   interpretation.
