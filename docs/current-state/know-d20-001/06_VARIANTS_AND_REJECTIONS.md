# Variants and Rejections

## Preserved variants

- BPHS category-start method: selected VEDA calculation method.
- Sequential destination-sign progression: VEDA evidence-qualified inference.
- Practitioner table and house-reading methods: discovery-only variant;
  not merged into the BPHS method.

## Rejected or deferred claims

- D20 equals spiritual maturity.
- D20 guarantees or measures moksha.
- D20 predicts sainthood, enlightenment, initiation or monkhood.
- D20 automatically identifies a deity, guru or successful practice.
- D20 house/planet recipes copied from modern lists.
- D20 as a generic career/dedication chart based on one practitioner article.

Conflict type: `PRACTITIONER_EXTENSION`, `MODERN_SIMPLIFICATION`, or
`UNRESOLVED` depending on claim. No incompatible traditions were flattened.
