# Source Research Method

## Priority and inspection

Primary evidence was inspected first in the accessible BPHS text pages for
Chapter 7 verses 1–5 and Chapter 6 verses 16–20. The relevant Sanskrit,
translation and surrounding verse context were read directly. Sanskrit
Documents was used as a catalogue for locating classical text editions, not
as proof of an uninspected interpretation.

Accepted primary evidence:

1. **Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra**, Chapter 7 verse 4, *Varga Viveka*:
   `सुखाऽसुखस्य विज्ञानं वाहनानां तथैव च। उपासनाया विज्ञानं साध्यं विंशतिभागके॥`
   The inspected translation assigns worship/upāsanā to Vimshamsha.
2. **Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra**, Chapter 6 verses 17–20:
   records twenty 1°30′ parts, category-based starting signs and deity lists.

Discovery-only or variant evidence:

- Shakti Mohan Singh, “Vinshansha or the D-20,” Saptarishis Astrology: useful
  for practitioner interpretation and a published table, not independent
  classical authority.
- modern D20 guides and SEO pages: rejected for promotion where they add
  moksha guarantees, house recipes, deity selection, initiation or maturity
  claims without passage-level provenance.

## Decision rule

The direct BPHS scope is promoted only as a narrow knowledge claim:

> Vimshamsha is source-associated with upāsanā/worship.

It is not expanded to “spiritual maturity,” “successful meditation,”
“moksha,” “renunciation,” “specific deity,” or “actual practice.”
