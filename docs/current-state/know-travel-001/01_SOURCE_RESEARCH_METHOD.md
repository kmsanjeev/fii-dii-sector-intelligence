# Source Research Method

Existing VEDA records were audited first. External research was targeted to the 25 requested claim families and prioritized named classical texts, a reliable Sanskrit/English presentation, a scholarly edition record, and lexical evidence. Search snippets, anonymous pages, unsourced tables, copied yoga lists, AI-looking articles and SEO pages were discovery-only or rejected.

Passage status vocabulary:

- `PASSAGE_VERIFIED`: actual page/section was accessed and the claim is supported at the stated scope.
- `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED`: source family or repeated claim was identified, but the exact passage/method was not verified.
- `TERMINOLOGY_ONLY`: lexical meaning is established, but no chart rule follows from it.
- `PLATFORM_EVIDENCE`: VEDA governance architecture, not a Sanskrit claim.

No Sanskrit verse is reproduced unless the accessed page displayed it. Most findings are paraphrased to avoid copyright and translation overclaiming. Historical geography is treated as a separate uncertainty layer: “abroad”, “distant” and “another country/kingdom” are not automatically modern nation-state categories.
