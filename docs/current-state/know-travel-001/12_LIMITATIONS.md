# Limitations

1. Passage coverage is partial. Hāyanaratna and Brihat Saṃhitā provide useful direct movement evidence, but most requested natal house/planet/Varga combinations remain unverified at passage level.
2. Hāyanaratna is an annual/Tājika work; its journey rules cannot be silently transplanted into a natal Parāśari runtime.
3. Lexical “foreign residence” is not immigration, citizenship, permanent settlement or a modern country mapping.
4. The audit did not establish a universal 12th-house settlement rule, a universal Rahu foreign rule, a universal return yoga, or duration thresholds.
5. D4 interpretation remains gated; D9/D10/D12 movement interpretation remains unvalidated.
6. Career, education and marriage relocation are cross-domain/platform associations, not modern legal or causal predictions.
7. Gendered historical role language must remain source-scoped and must not be generalized silently.
8. EMP-001 remains active longitudinal with insufficient sample. No fabricated cases were added. Predictive maturity remains `PRED-M3_OPERATIONAL_PLUS`.
