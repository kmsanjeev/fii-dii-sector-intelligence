# Limitations

- Official documentation reported 5,866 records and 4,832 A/AA; the observed
  July 2026 file contained 6,036 and 4,975. This may reflect revision or route
  differences and is not silently reconciled.
- ADB time fields are present for all records, but source metadata does not
  establish exact-minute/rounded precision. Birth precision is therefore
  `UNKNOWN`, not inferred from formatted clock text.
- 2,217 source-note records matched documentary keywords; this is a candidate
  screen, not documentary verification. 284 matched rectification-related
  keywords and remain non-documentary unless independently verified.
- A/AA is not VEDA Tier A/B. Birth and event provenance must be independently
  qualified; combined eligible subjects are zero in this audit.
- The C-name selection is not representative of the full database.
- The corroboration pilot sampled 30 candidate records and performed 6 bounded
  authoritative source checks; five matched exactly and one exposed a source
  conflict. It is not a full event audit.
- No raw provider data, names or biographies were committed.
