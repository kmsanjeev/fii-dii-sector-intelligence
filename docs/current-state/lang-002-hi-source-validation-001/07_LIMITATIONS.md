# Limitations

- This activity validates presentation wording and metadata boundaries, not
  Jyotisha doctrine, chart calculations, predictions, or life-event outcomes.
- Government and institutional references establish terminology/script and
  calendrical-reference classes; they do not substitute for human linguistic
  acceptance of the full UI.
- `SOURCE_REVIEWED` is not `HUMAN_REVIEWED`, `APPROVED_PRESENTATION`, or
  production authorization.
- Legacy Roman aliases are retained for compatibility and must not be treated
  as strict IAST.
- No RAG rebuild was necessary because no new semantic truth was introduced.
- No other language pack was created.
- The unrelated `data/reference/city_coords_cache.csv` edit remains outside
  this programme and must not be staged.
