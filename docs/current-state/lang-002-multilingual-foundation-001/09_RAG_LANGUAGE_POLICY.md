# RAG and Translated Knowledge Policy

No semantic governed knowledge changed in LANG-002. The unified RAG universe
and trust-zone counts are unchanged; no rebuild is required and no
language-specific retriever or corpus was created.

If a future translated classical proposition is stored, it must retain the
original `source_id`, canonical proposition ID, locale, translation status,
chapter/verse, confidence, and research zone. Translation does not create an
independent witness or promote a trust state.
