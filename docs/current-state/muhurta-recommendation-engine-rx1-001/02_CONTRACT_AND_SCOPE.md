# Contract and Scope

Activated contracts are exactly:

| Activity | Contract | Hash | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business | `VEDA-MUH-CONTRACT-BUSINESS-OPENING-V4` | `DBC0DF78...2584BE60` | Commercial-establishment opening/inauguration only |
| Education | `VEDA-MUH-CONTRACT-EDUCATION-COMMENCEMENT-V4` | `7A17DB6B...6805F35` | Formal course, programme, first lesson, or ceremonial commencement |

Business investment selection, stock trading, loans, financing, incorporation deadlines and tax filings are excluded. Education routine study, examinations, admission deadlines and mandatory school requirements are excluded. Religious, marriage, medical, legal and other unactivated subjects remain abstaining.

The Business Vara/Yoga and exact-opening Tithi gaps, and the Education Vara/Yoga and Karana gaps, are reported as nonblocking source gaps. They are not evaluated and do not force abstention when the available machine rules can be evaluated.
