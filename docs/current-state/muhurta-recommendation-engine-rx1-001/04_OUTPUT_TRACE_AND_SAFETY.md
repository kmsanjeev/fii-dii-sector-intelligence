# Output, Trace and Safety

Every evaluated result contains:

- activity and candidate window;
- categorical recommendation state;
- evaluated rules, support, adverse factors, requirements and source gaps;
- P032 calculation metadata;
- contract ID and full hash;
- engine ID/version, source assertion IDs and variant IDs;
- `personal_factors_evaluated=false`, with Tara Bala and Chandra Bala explicitly `NOT_EVALUATED`;
- mandatory general and activity-specific caution;
- consultation guidance;
- `capability_state=IMPLEMENTED_VALIDATED` and `access_state=ENABLED`;
- `trust_state=VALIDATED_CONTRACT_BOUND_GENERAL_ONLY`.

The output makes no guarantee, prediction, personal natal claim, religious/legal/financial/medical recommendation, or hidden ranking. No user or personal data is persisted or logged by the engine.
