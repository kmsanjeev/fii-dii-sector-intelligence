# Outcome Quality

PRED-001 verification states are retained: `SYSTEM_VERIFIED`, `MULTI_SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `DOCUMENT_VERIFIED`, `DATA_VERIFIED`, `USER_REPORTED`, and `UNVERIFIED`. Outcome rows remain separate from immutable prediction payloads. Weak or anecdotal outcomes must not be merged with verified outcomes without an explicit filter and disclosure.

The pilot creates no fake outcomes. Test outcomes use temporary databases and are not production evidence.
