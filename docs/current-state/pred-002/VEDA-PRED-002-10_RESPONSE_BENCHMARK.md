# Response Benchmark

The stable structural benchmark covers General, Career, Wealth, Education, Marriage, Progeny, Health, Timing, and AstroFinance. It measures chart/domain terms, evidence diversity, timing/prediction specificity, conflict visibility, generic and duplicate ratios, disclaimers, and trust-zone diversity. Benchmark definitions are separate from generated prose.

Provider-backed capture is conditional on configured provider availability. No provider output or human quality score is fabricated in this phase.
