# Temporary Research Boundary

Temporary external research remains separate from approved core.

- temporary external research is transient source data
- discovery-only material is not upgraded by retrieval rank
- chat continues to explain that outside research becomes durable only through review and promotion

Approved core may be compared against temporary research, but temporary research does not override governed knowledge silently.
