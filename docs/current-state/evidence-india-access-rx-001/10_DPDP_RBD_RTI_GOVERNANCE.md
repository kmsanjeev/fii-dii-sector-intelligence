# DPDP / RBD / RTI governance

The DPDP research/archiving/statistical conditions are not treated as a disclosure entitlement. The Registration of Births and Deaths framework governs registration and certification; it does not by itself authorize bulk release. RTI is limited here to metadata. Any real-data route requires current legal review, purpose limitation, minimisation, identity custody, security, retention and approved access conditions. No categorical claim that data is automatically illegal or automatically releasable is made.
