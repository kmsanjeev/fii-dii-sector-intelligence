# Spiritual Term Governance

| Term | Literal/contextual reading | Jyotisha use allowed | Modern overextension risk | Final status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dharma | duty, conduct, order, fortune/context of the 9th; not a synonym for all spirituality | 9th-house dharma/fortune context | reducing dharma to “spirituality” | `VALIDATED_KNOWLEDGE` at narrow lexical level; rule use conditional |
| Moksha | release/liberation in religious-philosophical usage | non-deterministic orientation only | claiming attainment | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Vairagya | dispassion/detachment from objects | detachment orientation | depression, poverty or failure | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Sannyasa | renounced order/formal ascetic life; distinct from inner detachment | formal-renunciation tendency only under exact method | casual “monk destiny” | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Sadhana | disciplined practice | practice orientation when separately evidenced | assuming actual practice | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Tapas | austerity/discipline/penance | disciplined effort, not glorified suffering | romanticising deprivation | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Bhakti | devotion/worship | devotional mode | equating emotion with devotion | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Jnana | knowledge/wisdom | higher-knowledge/study orientation | equating study with realization | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Guru/ācārya | teacher/preceptor | teacher orientation | predicting a particular guru | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Mantra/upāsanā | mantra; worship/meditative practice | D20 source scope: upāsanā | claiming initiation or practice | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| Tyāga | relinquishment/renunciation in broader religious/philosophical context | not used as a binary chart result | flattening to sannyasa | `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED` |

Translation uncertainty is material for moksha, vairagya, pravrajya, sannyasa, tyaga and upasana. Exact Sanskrit is recorded only where the cited passage was inspected.
