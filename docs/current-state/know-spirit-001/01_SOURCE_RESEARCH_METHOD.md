# Source Research Method

The audit prioritised actual classical text passages, then reliable Sanskrit/translation pages, then traditional or practitioner material for variant discovery. Search snippets, SEO pages, unsourced yoga lists and modern spirituality articles were not treated as authority.

Accepted primary evidence:

- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapter 7 verses 1-5: divisional purposes; Vimshamsha is assigned to `upāsanā`.
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapter 6 verses 17-20: Vimshamsha division, category-based commencement and deity lists.
- Brihat Jataka, Chapter 15 verses 1-4: Pravrajya/renunciation combinations.
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapter 79, verses 1-15: a separate renunciation-yoga tradition, retained as a source variant pending edition verification.
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapters 16, 20 and 23: house-topic context for the 5th, 9th and 12th houses.

No claim is promoted when an exact passage, edition or method cannot be verified. Such claims remain `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED` or `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`.
