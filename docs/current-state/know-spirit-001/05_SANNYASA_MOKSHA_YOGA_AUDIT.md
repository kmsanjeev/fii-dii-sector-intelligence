# Sannyasa and Moksha Yoga Audit

## Sannyasa / Pravrajya

Brihat Jataka Chapter 15 contains four verses on Pravrajya Yoga. Its first verse describes multiple strong planets occupying one sign and distinguishes ascetic types by the strongest planet; subsequent verses address combustion, Moon/ascendant/planet relationships and a dharma-house configuration. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra Chapter 79, as represented by a practitioner-hosted translation, contains a 15-verse Pravrajya Yoga chapter with four-or-more-strong-planet conditions, strength, combustion, planetary war, cancellation/abandonment and dasha activation.

These are not merged. Edition lineage and chapter numbering differ, and the BPHS Chapter 79 source used for discovery is not sufficient by itself for classical promotion. The dedicated matrix is therefore:

| Method | Source | Requirements | Cancellation/qualification | Authority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BJ-PRAVRAJYA-15 | Brihat Jataka 15.1-4 | multiple strong planets and source-specific configuration | source-specific defeat/combustion/context clauses | Classical passage verified | `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` |
| BPHS-PRAVRAJYA-79 | BPHS Ch.79, 15 verses; translation reference inspected | four or more strong planets and additional variants | combustion, planetary war, strength and dasha clauses | Reference/traditional source | `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED` pending edition verification |

No sannyasa method is activated in P031. No “formal renunciation” output is permitted.

## Moksha

No inspected Jyotisha passage justifies a guaranteed liberation output. Moksha remains a religious-philosophical concept and, at most, a non-deterministic orientation label. Popular “moksha yoga” lists without passage-level provenance are rejected or retained as `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`.
