# Limitations

- Passage verification is limited to accessible editions and translations; edition lineage can change verse numbering.
- BPHS D20 source evidence establishes upasana scope and category starts, but the inspected source does not by itself settle every modern sign/deity mapping convention.
- Practitioner D20 and Pravrajya material is variant discovery, not promotion evidence.
- No exact universal moksha-yoga or Ketu-past-life rule was verified.
- Classical gender, varna, ashrama and sectarian contexts are not universalized.
- No clinical, religious-superiority, guru-selection or formal-renunciation output is authorized.
