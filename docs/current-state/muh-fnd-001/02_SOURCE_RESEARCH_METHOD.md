# Source Research Method

## Source hierarchy

Primary evidence was prioritized from Varahamihira's *Brihat Samhita*, using the
[Sanskrit text and edition notes hosted by Sanskrit Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/bRRihatsaMhitA.html).
The [Varahamihira catalogue](https://sanskritdocuments.org/iast/varahamihira/)
was used to identify available text and scan variants. Chapters 97-99 provide
nakshatra, tithi and karana action classifications; Chapter 102 contains
marriage-specific material including the godhuli/goraraja passage.

The source is used as a classical rule-family anchor, not as proof that every
modern Muhurta convention is classical. The edition page identifies Sanskrit
text/translation and commentary/edition lineage; exact translation differences
remain possible.

Secondary discovery sources for Tarabala, Chandrabala and modern time-window
conventions were not promoted without direct passage verification. The
Muhurta Chintamani scan and practitioner manuals remain `REFERENCE_NOT_VERIFIED`
for this activity.

## Evidence policy

- Passage verified: exact chapter/passage was inspected in an accessible text.
- Reference only: a source is named or discoverable, but the operative passage
  was not independently verified.
- Research candidate: plausible traditional method requiring further audit.
- Platform evidence: deterministic safety/readiness logic, not classical proof.
- Rejected: popular universal claim without provenance or with category error.
