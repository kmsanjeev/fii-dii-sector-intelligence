## Source-scoped rules

- Nakshatra, tithi and karana suitability may be represented as event-specific
  rule families, not as a universal auspiciousness score.
- The godhuli/goraraja passage is a marriage-context exception and must not be
  generalized to all events.
- No Tarabala, Chandrabala, Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika, Abhijit or
  Durmuhurta method is promoted in this audit without passage-level validation.
