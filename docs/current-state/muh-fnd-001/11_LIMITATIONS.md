# Limitations

- The inspected classical evidence establishes rule families, not a complete
  modern electional software specification.
- Tarabala and Chandrabala remain reference-level until their source passages
  and variants are directly verified.
- Historical regional practice, sunrise conventions and local calendars may
  differ.
- No claim here ranks traditions or promises outcomes.
- This activity does not provide legal, medical, financial or religious advice.
- A future implementation must expose missing data and source authority rather
  than silently returning “auspicious.”
