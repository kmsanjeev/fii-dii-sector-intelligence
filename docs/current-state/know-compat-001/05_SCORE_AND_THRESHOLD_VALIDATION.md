# Score and Threshold Validation

The eight-component maxima reported by SRC-KC-01 and SRC-KC-02 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8, totaling 36. This validates the existence of the inspected 36-point practitioner framework, not a passage-level classical origin for every table.

The Future Samachar reference discusses 18 as a suggested minimum and an alternative 21 condition (PDF pp20-21). These are classified `MODERN_CONVENTION` / `PRACTITIONER_METHOD`, not Approved Core. Popular 24, 28 and 32 thresholds were not verified as classical in this audit and remain unverified claims. No threshold is encoded as a relationship verdict.

P028 must present the traditional score separately from broader D1/D9, timing, individual-promise and directional synthesis. A high score is not a guarantee; a low score is not a doomed relationship.
