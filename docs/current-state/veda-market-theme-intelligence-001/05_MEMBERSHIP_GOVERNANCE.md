# Membership Governance

`APPROVED_STRUCTURED_CLASSIFICATION` is the registry authority. Methods are
`DETERMINISTIC_CLASSIFICATION` and `DETERMINISTIC_CROSS_THEME`; membership is
not generated at request time by an LLM. Missing evidence remains missing.

The contract does not infer membership from a company mention, product
announcement, optionality, a sector name, or a keyword. Candidate additions
remain outside the active set until an evidence-backed source and method are
recorded.
