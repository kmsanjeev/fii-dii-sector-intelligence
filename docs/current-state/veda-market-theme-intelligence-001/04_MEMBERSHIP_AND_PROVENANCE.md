# Membership and Provenance

Active membership is deterministically derived from `theme_tagging.csv` rows
whose source is `classification_v4` or `cross_theme` and whose theme is in the
governed registry. Classification rows are `PRIMARY/CORE` with high quality;
cross-theme rows are `CROSS_THEME` with material/secondary exposure and medium
quality.

Keyword-only rows, cap-factor rows and unrelated legacy themes are not active
membership evidence in this contract. They remain available to their legacy
consumer and are not deleted.

The source date is `2026-06-30`. Effective-to is unknown and is therefore null.
The contract states that current membership is used for all windows and that
historical membership snapshots are unavailable.
