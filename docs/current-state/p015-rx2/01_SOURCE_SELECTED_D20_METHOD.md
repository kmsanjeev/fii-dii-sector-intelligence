Source: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Ch.6.17–20 and Ch.7.4.

The inspected passage states Vimshamsha as 20 parts of 1°30′ and gives starts from Aries for movable signs, Sagittarius for fixed signs, and Leo for dual/common signs. Ch.7.4 assigns Vimshamsha a worship/upasana scope. The passage also lists deity assignments. It does not fully specify a modern destination-sign mapping for the returned sign contract. Sequential progression from the category start is therefore implemented as an explicit evidence-qualified inference, not represented as a fully source-verified deity/sign equivalence.
