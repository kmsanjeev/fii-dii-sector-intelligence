# Data governance statement

VEDA will use Astro-Databank material only under Astrodienst's written terms
and only for the approved research purpose. Access, fields, retention,
attribution, redistribution, and publication will be limited to what the
provider explicitly permits.

Raw provider records are kept local/ignored, are not committed to Git, and are
not redistributed. No identity re-identification, unsupported identifier
mapping, scraping, or bulk extraction is authorized. Provider record
identifiers and any licensed birth fields will be handled as restricted
research inputs. VEDA will retain a provenance/terms register and publish only
aggregate or otherwise provider-permitted results.

No access request has been submitted, no license has been accepted, and no
provider data was acquired in this activity. The Founder must review and fill
the action card before any external contact.
