# VEDA research summary for provider review

## Project

VEDA is an independent, governed research platform for testing narrowly
specified astrological hypotheses under explicit provenance, source-quality,
date-precision, and statistical controls. This request concerns lawful birth
data provenance and access conditions only. It does not ask Astrodienst to
validate astrology or to endorse VEDA conclusions.

## Current bounded state

- The governed birth frame contains 114 A/B subjects from prior authorized
  sample work; raw provider records remain local and ignored.
- The exact-day POSITION_END pilot contains 4 subjects, with formal role
  start/end dates at DAY precision.
- The feature family and matched case-crossover protocol are frozen before any
  future scoring; feature activation has not been inspected.
- The holdout remains unopened.
- The current four-subject corpus is design-feasibility evidence only. VEDA has
  made no positive signal, effect, correlation, or predictive claim.
- The next POSEND R2 frame is blocked because the existing frame is exhausted
  and meaningful independent scale requires lawful formal access.

## Why access is relevant

The requested route is to evaluate whether provider-authorized birth-record
lineage and reliability metadata can support a broader, independent,
provenance-qualified frame. POSITION_END event dates are independently verified
from official government, parliamentary, or institutional documents. ADB birth
data is not automatically treated as event or outcome authority.

## Requested scope

Please advise which currently permitted route can provide the minimum lawful
research access and provenance metadata needed for this project. VEDA is not
requesting a full export while the public 2026 export route is suspended, and
does not assume that research-tool subscription provides export or bulk rights.
