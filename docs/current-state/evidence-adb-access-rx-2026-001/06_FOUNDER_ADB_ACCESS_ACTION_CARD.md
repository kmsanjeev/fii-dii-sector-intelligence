# Founder action card: Astro-Databank access

## Fill in exactly

1. Founder legal/name and contact email.
2. Truthful researcher identity and qualification wording.
3. Affiliation, if one exists; otherwise say independent researcher.
4. Jurisdiction and any real publication/research links.
5. The provider-directed contact channel confirmed from the current official
   site.

Do not invent credentials, publications, institutional affiliation, funding,
provider approval, or access rights.

## Attach

- `02_VEDA_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md`
- `03_DATA_GOVERNANCE_STATEMENT.md`
- `04_AI_ML_NONTRAINING_STATEMENT.md`
- the prepared POSEND protocol/status summary, with no raw provider records
- any truthful Founder CV/publication links, if available

Do not attach raw ADB records, ignored local files, unreviewed provider
exports, or identity-resolution material.

## Use the official route

Start at the official ADB export readme and research-tool pages. Ask which
permitted route applies: interactive research-tool subscription, qualified-
researcher permission, or another provider-directed route. The 2026 public
full-export route is suspended; do not ask for or attempt the removed ZIP.

## Request

Request only lawful research access and the minimum provenance/terms metadata
needed for the project. Do not assume export, API, programmatic, bulk, or
redistribution rights. Ask the provider to state them in writing.

## Claims to avoid

- no claim that VEDA has provider approval;
- no claim that the four-person pilot found a signal;
- no claim that ADB birth data establishes POSITION_END outcomes;
- no claim of a full export, API, bulk download, or license;
- no claim that credentials/publications exist unless the Founder supplies them;
- no AI/ML training, RAG, scraping, reidentification, or redistribution claim.

## After submission

The human must record the provider response, exact terms, route, permitted
fields, retention, publication and export restrictions, and whether further
legal/privacy review is required. Until that record exists, formal access is
blocked and POSEND R2 remains not started.
