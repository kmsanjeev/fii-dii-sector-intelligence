# Source and Trust Governance

The prior foundation audit established scoped classical rule families from
Varahamihira's *Brihat Samhita* Chapters 97–99 and 102. Those rules remain
knowledge-level evidence and are not silently converted into executable
recommendations here.

The solar-day calculation is engineering foundation evidence. Tarabala,
Chandrabala and regional time-window conventions remain research candidates or
reference-level until independently verified. No Approved Core promotion or
new trust zone occurs.
