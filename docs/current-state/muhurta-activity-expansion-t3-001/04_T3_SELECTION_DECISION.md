# T3 Selection Decision

Decision: `MUHURTA_T3_ACTIVITY_EXPANSION_SOURCE_CONTRACTS_MACHINE_PARTIAL`.

One activity was selected: `MARRIAGE_CEREMONY_TIMING`.

It has the strongest remaining source-to-action mapping. Bṛhat Saṃhitā,
chapter 103, contains a marriage-time Godhūli context, and
Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, marriage prakarana, records marriage-specific Lagna and
planetary-strength context. These sources justify a bounded timing contract,
not partner matching, a decision whether to marry, or a marriage-outcome
claim.

No second activity was forced. Travel remains scope-ambiguous because the
available evidence does not yet define journey subtype and direction in a
machine-safe contract. Puja/Japa/Vrata requires separate ceremony-specific
primary passages and lineage variants. Property and medical activities retain
legal/practical and safety blockers. House activities remain excluded by the
T2 freeze.

The selected activity is `SOURCE_CONTRACT_READY_MACHINE_PARTIAL`, not
`MACHINE_CONTRACT_READY`: validated electional Lagna and planetary factors and
a governed Godhūli/sunset factor are not available from the trusted P032
contract. Consequently no T3 engine handoff is executable.

Selection was qualitative only: passage strength, scope clarity, factor
feasibility, calculation availability, conflict burden, context burden and
utility. No numeric score, weighting, ranking or probability was used.
