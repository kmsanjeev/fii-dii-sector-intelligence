# Selected Activity A — Marriage Ceremony Timing Source Audit

## Scope

The normalized scope is timing of a human-chosen marriage ceremony or
panigrahana event. Marriage compatibility, partner selection, whether people
should marry, legal registration and outcome prediction are excluded.

## Accepted lineage

1. **Bṛhat Saṃhitā, Vivāha-paṭala, chapter 103, verse 13** — the consulted
   N. Chidambaram Iyer translation witness records the distinct Godhūli
   marriage context and its exceptional relationship to ordinary Panchanga
   and Lagna considerations. This is a bounded source assertion, not a
   generic sunset calculator.
2. **Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, marriage prakarana 6, digitized 1945 edition, printed
   p. 233 / scan page 249** — the consulted edition records bride/groom
   Guru/Sun/Moon strength and examination of marriage Lagna. The source's
   historical social and outcome language is retained as context only.
3. **Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, chapter 6, verse 76, secondary manuscript-tradition
   comparison** — marriage-time Lagna/Navamsha relationship language is
   recorded as source context. It is not silently converted into a production
   predicate because VEDA has not validated the required electional
   calculation dependency or variant priority.

The complete witness metadata is in `05_SOURCE_RESEARCH_REGISTER.json`.
No Sanskrit verse is reproduced here; no OCR-only claim is accepted.
