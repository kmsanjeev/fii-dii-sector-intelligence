- 2,434 symbols have announcement and event-calendar coverage;
- 3,327 symbols occur in normalized corporate actions;
- 2,333 symbols occur in quarterly results;
- 5-symbol live sample (`RELIANCE`, `TCS`, `INFY`, `HDFCBANK`, `SBIN`) resolved

- the sample demonstrated unknown categories, management/regulatory/credit,

Coverage is not completeness. Unknown source types remain unknown, source
freshness can be stale/unknown, and future scheduled dates remain contextual.
No claim is made that every issuer, event lifecycle or completion state is
covered.
