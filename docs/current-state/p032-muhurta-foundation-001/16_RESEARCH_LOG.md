## External/source pass

Queries were limited to locating primary/traditional text witnesses and
chapter context for Bṛhat Saṃhitā chapters 97-100. The consulted translation
index identifies the asterism, lunar-day and karana function chapters. A
classical-muhurta secondary translation was used only to understand the scope
of action-family terminology, not as an executable universal score.

The accessed chapter index was the Ramakrishna Bhat translation landing page:
`https://astroamerica.com/text/brihatsamhita.html`. The Sanskrit Documents
text witness was used for text availability only:
`https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/bRRihatsaMhitA.html`.

The existing repository records also document direct inspection of the
Muhurta Chintamani candidate PDF/metadata witnesses. OCR was visibly
fragmented, so no Tara Bala or Chandra Bala formula was reconstructed.

- Any recommendation, ranking, or user-outcome claim.
