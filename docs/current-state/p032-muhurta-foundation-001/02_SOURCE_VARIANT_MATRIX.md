Chapter numbering varies across editions and translations. The registry uses
source-family locators rather than inventing verse numbers. The consulted
online translation confirms that the Bṛhat Saṃhitā contents include functions
of asterisms, lunar days and karanas; the implementation does not claim that
this establishes a universal modern scoring system.
