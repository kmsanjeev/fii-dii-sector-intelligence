# Leakage, Privacy and Case Gate

An eligible case requires a source-backed subject/chart record, documented
birth-data quality, an objectively dateable event, independent verification,
case-family/source-family controls and complete chronology where applicable.

Historical cases without prediction and knowledge cutoffs remain research-only.
Prospective cases require consent or a legitimate public-observation basis,
must be frozen before the outcome, and must not be edited after resolution.
Private or identifying information must not be added merely to increase sample
size.
