# Personal-factor composition

Tara and Chandra remain separate qualitative facts. No numeric combination
score, ranking weight, or priority rule is introduced.

The diagnostic composition states are:

- `BOTH_SUPPORTIVE`
- `ONE_FACTOR_SUPPORTIVE`
- `PERSONAL_CAUTION`
- `MIXED_PERSONAL_FACTORS`
- `PERSONAL_FACTOR_UNAVAILABLE`
- `PERSONAL_FACTOR_UNCERTAIN`

Future personalization must retain `GENERAL_RESULT`, `PERSONAL_RESULT`, and
`FINAL_PERSONALIZED_RESULT`. Personal caution cannot erase a general result, and
missing personal data cannot disable general Muhurta.
