# India External-Review Checklist

Status: `INDIA_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_READY_FOR_COUNSEL`, not legally approved.

- Map digital-personal-data processing to the DPDP Act, notified Rules and
  applicable commencement dates.
- Confirm notice, consent, withdrawal, access/correction, retention, deletion,
  grievance/contact and processor/vendor obligations with counsel.
- Determine whether any event data or research framing brings the protocol
  within ICMR human-participant/health-research ethics review.
- Determine CERT-In direction applicability, incident process and reporting
  obligations for the entity and hosting environment.
- Review India language/accessibility, sensitive document handling and
  cross-border/cloud transfers.
- Decide controller/processor roles, identity-vault location and backup policy.

Other jurisdictions are out of scope and require separate counsel review.
