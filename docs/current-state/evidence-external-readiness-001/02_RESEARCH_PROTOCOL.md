# Research Protocol Draft

Purpose: assess whether a consented longitudinal evidence corpus can be
designed ethically and securely to study source-ranked event data. It does not
claim predictive validity and does not authorize chart calculation, outcome
prediction or clinical, death, legal, financial or immigration advice.

Population is adults only by default. Participation is voluntary, unpaid unless
externally approved, and may be stopped without penalty. Research is feature-
blind at intake, uses minimum necessary event descriptions, separates birth and
event provenance, and permits granular refusal and withdrawal.

No recruitment may begin until the gate matrix, counsel review, independent
ethics review, security review, identity-vault decision and provider permissions
are resolved.
