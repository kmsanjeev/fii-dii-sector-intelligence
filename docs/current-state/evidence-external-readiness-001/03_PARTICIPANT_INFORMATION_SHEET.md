# Participant Information Sheet — Draft, Not for Distribution

VEDA is a research project studying the quality and timing of historical
records. Predictive validity is not established. Participation is voluntary and
can be withdrawn. The project may request birth details, documentary evidence,
and dated life-event information because evidence quality and date precision are
research variables.

VEDA will not provide medical, legal or financial advice, make death claims, or
promise personal predictions. It will not calculate astrology for participants
as part of this readiness phase. Optional uses—follow-up, document submission,
publication and deidentified sharing—are separate consent choices.

Contact, complaint route, retention periods, controller/processor identity and
jurisdiction-specific rights remain placeholders pending legal and ethics review.
No document should be distributed before those fields are approved.
