# Source Research Method

Primary source: Varahamihira, *Brihat Samhita*, Sanskrit text based on the A.V.
Tripathi Sarasvati Bhavan Granthamala edition, with Kern variants and Utpala
commentary markers as documented by the host.

The operative passages inspected were Chapters 97, 98 and 99. The source page
also identifies the digitization and edition lineage. Exact translation is not
used as a substitute for the inspected Sanskrit passage.

The accessible 23-page Muhurta Chintamani Chapter 5 PDF was inspected directly.
Its OCR is visibly corrupted in relevant portions; it is retained as a
traditional candidate source, not as passage-level proof of personal Bala
formulas.

Modern websites, social posts and copied tables were rejected as primary
authority.
