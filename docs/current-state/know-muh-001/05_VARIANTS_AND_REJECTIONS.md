# Variants and Rejections

## Preserved variants

- Classical nakshatra/tithi/karana action classes are retained as event-scoped
  families.
- Marriage-specific conditions are not generalized to travel, education or
  business.
- Regional and later Muhurta traditions remain separate from Varahamihira.

## Rejected or deferred claims

- “Any favorable Panchanga produces an auspicious event.”
- “Vishti always denies every activity.”
- “Tarabala and Chandrabala formulas are verified because modern tables repeat.”
- “One classical passage defines a universal electional score.”
- “A source-backed event class guarantees an outcome.”
