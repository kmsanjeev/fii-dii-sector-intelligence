# T2 selection decision

Selected activities:

1. `HOUSE_CONSTRUCTION_COMMENCEMENT` — the source material explicitly covers
   entering a prepared site, commencing building work, and laying the first
   foundation stone. The scope is narrower than property purchase or legal
   construction approval.
2. `HOUSE_ENTRY_OR_GRIHA_PRAVESHA` — the existing source witness connects
   house entry with the same bounded house-commencement context, while the
   exact election requires Lagna and planetary conditions not exposed by the
   current trusted factor contract.

The selection uses qualitative source strength, direct source-to-activity
mapping, reuse of existing P032 factors, bounded practical scope, and utility.
No numeric readiness score, ranking, weighting, or averaging was used.

Marriage was not selected despite strong source references because its
high-consequence, tradition-specific election semantics remain unresolved.
Travel, ritual, property transaction, and medical candidates retain their T1
scope or safety blockers.

Both selected activities reach `SOURCE_CONTRACT_READY_MACHINE_PARTIAL`, not
machine-ready. T2 therefore creates no executable engine handoff and does not
authorize runtime expansion.
