## Narrow falsifiable question

`EMP025-MP-Q01`: Does governed Dasha timing discriminate public-role
transition events from matched time-window controls?

The pilot's event subset is the 15-case union of `POSITION_START`,
`POSITION_END`, `ELECTION_WIN` and `PUBLIC_APPOINTMENT` in the frozen
25-case corpus. The question is intentionally narrower than a general
prediction claim and does not include death-event inference.

## Primary methods and exclusions

Primary methods are existing D1 chart facts and governed Vimshottari Dasha
timing. The pilot must use the frozen subject-level DESIGN/VALIDATION/HOLDOUT
split and the existing matched time-window control specification. BAV, SAV,
unvalidated interpretive features, chart-based candidate selection and
holdout tuning are excluded. The holdout is protected until the method and
decision rule are frozen.

This is a handoff, not a result: no hit rate, significance claim or predictive
maturity change is asserted here. `PRED-M4` remains insufficient sample.
