# Limitations

- The cohort is small and source-cluster concentrated; it is not
  representative of countries, eras, occupations or the general population.
- The records are public-figure sports biographies, producing documentation and
  fame bias. Country counts are US 5, France 12 and Italy 3; India is 0.
- All event dates are YEAR precision. No exact-day confirmatory or month-level
  secondary subject is available after the bounded review.
- Birth provenance is recorded separately as reviewed OGDB timed records. It is
  not evidence that event dates are documentary or exact.
- Risk intervals are unavailable because governed role-start dates are absent.
- Claude Abbes has an unresolved event-definition conflict between the acquired
  1962 label and the FFF page's club chronology through June 1967.
- The existing acquisition controls are prepared but unscored and cannot be
  converted into final dates for this cohort.
- The feature family remains metadata-frozen and unassessed for activation.
- No claim about predictive validity, astrology, ML or PRED-M4 is made.
- Formal Astro-Databank access remains a parallel prepared/unsent external
  action; this activity does not submit a request or ingest provider data.
