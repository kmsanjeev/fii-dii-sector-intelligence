# POSITION_END Reacquisition Plan

Reacquisition is required but is not started by this activity.

## Priority order

1. `PUBLIC_OFFICE_END` or `FORMAL_APPOINTMENT_END` with an official effective
   date and an identifiable role start.
2. `RETIREMENT_EFFECTIVE_DATE` with an official retirement notice or equivalent
   institutional record.
3. `RESIGNATION_EFFECTIVE_DATE` with an acceptance/effective-date record.
4. `TERM_COMPLETION` where the term and office are explicitly documented.

Candidates must be ranked by objectivity, source availability, event-definition
clarity, DAY precision, independent birth-data overlap, ethical risk and source
independence. Do not select candidates because of a feature fit.

## Entry gates

Each future subject requires separate birth and event provenance, a stable
event ID, one pre-specified event rule, an explicit effective date, a usable
at-risk interval, a declared source-dependence cluster and a subject-level
validation/holdout assignment. Any YEAR or MONTH record remains outside the
confirmatory DAY cohort.

The existing six-subject holdout must remain sealed and must not be replaced by
convenient records. Future acquisition must not merge this lane with the
separate public-appointment corpus without a new semantic and independence
decision.
