The standard infers language, code-switching, intent, tone, emotion, formality, directness, humour, relationship context, and domain proficiency without false certainty. Group turn dynamics remain a foundation for `VEDA-GROUP-001`. Understanding slang does not mean blindly mirroring slang. Implementation evidence is in `docs/current-state/std-003/`.
