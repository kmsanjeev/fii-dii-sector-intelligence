# VEDA-STD-003 Capture

**Title:** Universal Conversational Intelligence, Pragmatics & Multilingual Expression Standard
**Status:** CAPTURED / PLANNED / NOT_IMPLEMENTED

Scope includes conversation type, interpersonal pragmatics, small talk, heart-to-heart, pillow talk, sweet talk, pep talk, real talk, straight talk, trash talk, double talk, shop talk, and future extensible styles such as sarcasm, banter, debate, negotiation, humour, flirting, persuasion, consoling, venting, storytelling, mentoring, and diplomacy.

Wave 1 languages: English, Hindi, Hinglish. Knowledge forms: idioms, phrases, slang, colloquialisms, proverbs, metaphors, abbreviations, internet slang, professional jargon, and regional expressions.

The standard must infer language, code-switching, intent, tone, emotion, formality, directness, humour, relationship context, domain proficiency, and group turn dynamics without false certainty. Understanding slang does not mean blindly mirroring slang.
